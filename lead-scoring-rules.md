# Lead Scoring Rules

## Purpose

Lead scoring helps the CRM decide which leads are most valuable and ready for sales follow-up.

## Example Scoring Model

```text
Budget above £1,000: +20 points
Decision maker: +20 points
Needs service within 30 days: +15 points
Company website available: +10 points
Opened email: +5 points
Booked a call: +30 points
No response after 14 days: -10 points
```

## Score Categories

```text
0–30: Cold Lead
31–60: Warm Lead
61–80: Qualified Lead
81–100: Hot Lead
```
