# Lead Capture Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow captures new leads from multiple sources and sends them into the CRM automatically.

## Lead Sources

- Website forms
- Landing pages
- Facebook Lead Ads
- Google Ads forms
- LinkedIn outreach
- Chatbots
- Referrals
- Appointment booking forms

## Automation Flow

```text
Lead submits form
→ Zapier or n8n receives lead data
→ Contact is created in HubSpot or GoHighLevel
→ Lead source is added
→ Sales team is notified
→ Follow-up sequence starts
```

## SaaS Tools Used

- HubSpot
- GoHighLevel
- Zapier
- n8n
- Slack
- Google Sheets
- Email/SMS tools
