# Retention Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow keeps active customers engaged and reduces churn.

## Automation Flow

```text
Customer becomes active
→ Monthly check-in sequence starts
→ Usage data is monitored
→ Renewal date is tracked
→ Customer health score is updated
→ At-risk customers are flagged
```

## Retention Actions

- Monthly check-in emails
- Product tips
- Usage reports
- Renewal reminders
- Customer health scoring
- Account manager tasks

## SaaS Tools Used

- HubSpot
- GoHighLevel
- n8n
- Zapier
- Slack
- Email automation
