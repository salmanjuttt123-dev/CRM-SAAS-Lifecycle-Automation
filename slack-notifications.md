# Slack Notifications

## Purpose

Slack is used to notify internal teams when important CRM events happen.

## Notification Examples

```text
New qualified lead created
Deal moved to Proposal Sent
Deal marked as Won
Customer health score drops below 50
Support ticket becomes urgent
Renewal date is approaching
```

## Example Automation

```text
HubSpot deal marked as Won
→ Zapier sends Slack notification
→ Customer success team starts onboarding
```
