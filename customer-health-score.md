# Customer Health Score

## Purpose

Customer health scoring helps identify active, at-risk, and high-value customers.

## Health Score Factors

```text
Recent login activity
Product usage
Support ticket volume
Payment status
NPS score
Email engagement
Renewal date
Feature adoption
```

## Example Health Categories

```text
80–100: Healthy Customer
50–79: Needs Attention
0–49: At Risk
```

## Automation Logic

```text
IF customer health score falls below 50
THEN notify customer success
AND create follow-up task
AND send helpful reactivation email
```
